# AI-Assisted QA Workflow

## Where an assistant can help

- Turn approved requirements into candidate test ideas and edge cases.
- Draft a test matrix, then check it against the source requirements.
- Improve the clarity and consistency of a bug report.
- Group repeated findings and identify questions for the product owner.

## What the tester owns

- Confirming requirements and expected results with the product owner.
- Choosing risk and severity based on user impact and evidence.
- Reproducing defects and recording actual results.
- Reviewing every AI suggestion for correctness, privacy, and scope.
- Deciding whether a release meets its acceptance criteria.

## Example prompt pattern

“Using only the attached approved acceptance criteria, propose positive, negative, boundary, accessibility, and bilingual test cases. Mark assumptions and questions separately. Do not invent prices, business rules, or expected outcomes.”

## Evidence rule

AI-generated content is a draft. A case is not “tested” until a human executes it in the stated environment and records the actual result. Do not send customer information, credentials, private prompts, or production records to an unapproved AI service.
